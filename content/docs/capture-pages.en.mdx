---
title: Capture Pages
description: Publish public forms and measure conversions.
---

Capture pages publish a public form at `/capture/{slug}`. They collect contacts without an external form tool.

## Configuration

A page can include a title, description, configurable fields, published or draft status, and conversion settings.

## Collected data

Submissions create or enrich organization contacts. Standard and custom fields can be used depending on the form.

## Analytics

The page dashboard shows views, conversions, and daily stats to measure performance.
