## Available modes

MailPulse supports two organization-level modes:

- Meta Cloud API, recommended for production.
- WhatsApp Web through Evolution API and Baileys, useful for setup or troubleshooting.

## Meta Cloud API

Meta is the official channel. It requires a WhatsApp Business Account, a phone number, a token, a `phone_number_id`, and approved templates for messages outside the 24-hour window.

## Baileys and Evolution API

The QR code mode connects a WhatsApp Web session. It is simple, but unofficial. The session can disconnect, and high-volume usage can expose the number to restrictions or suspension.

## MailPulse resource

When `MAILPULSE_MANAGED_WHATSAPP_ENABLED=true`, MailPulse can provide a platform resource. It helps users without a number, but it is less personalized and subject to platform limits.

## Sending

The `WhatsApp` page lets users activate a mode, scan a QR code, save Meta configuration, send a single message, send bulk messages, and disconnect the session.

## Recipient consent and pacing

An external application can require the recipient's explicit agreement before
writing to them: MailPulse first sends the request written by the application,
keeps the content encrypted, then sends it after a YES or cancels it after a NO
or STOP. A recipient who said NO or STOP receives nothing more from that
application through this number, until a new YES. The command and event details
are in the API reference.

On WhatsApp Web, these sends go through a queue specific to each number, to limit
the risk of the number being blocked:

- a random pause of 15 to 45 seconds separates two messages;
- at most 40 first consent requests per day and per number;
- nothing is sent between 9 pm and 7 am, in the number's time zone (`Africa/Abidjan` by default).

These three settings can be changed per number with the provisioning script
(`--daily-consent-limit`, `--quiet-hours`, `--time-zone`). Recipients who already
agreed go before new requests. Meta applies its own limits and is not subject to
this queue.

To receive delivery and read receipts, the Evolution session must send the
`MESSAGES_UPDATE` events in addition to `MESSAGES_UPSERT`.
