## Campaigns

Campaign analytics consolidate delivered, opened, clicked, bounced, complained, and unsubscribed. Rates are recalculated after tracking events and Resend webhooks.

## Dashboard

The dashboard shows active contacts, campaign volume, sends, recent events, and plan usage. Convex powers live stats, presence, and notifications.

## Product

PostHog collects product events used to improve the application. Sensitive events and secrets must never be sent to product analytics.
