## Contacts

The `Contacts` page lets an organization create, edit, and inspect contacts. A contact can include email, first name, last name, phone, subscription status, tags, and custom field values.

## CSV import

CSV import creates multiple contacts in one operation. Plan limits are enforced during import to avoid exceeding contact quotas.

## Tags

Tags organize the audience by source, event, interest, sales pipeline, or campaign. They can be reused in segments and automations.

## Custom fields

Custom fields add business data to contacts. Supported types include text, number, email, URL, date, select, and boolean.

## Segments

Segments dynamically resolve contacts from rules such as subscription status, engagement score, dates, tags, and available data. They are used as campaign audiences and automation inputs.
