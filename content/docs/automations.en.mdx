MailPulse includes a visual automation editor based on nodes and edges. Workflows can be draft, active, paused, or archived.

## Triggers

Supported triggers include subscriber added, tag added, campaign opened, link clicked, date based, and custom event.

## Actions

Actions depend on available organization resources: email, tags, delays, conditions, and communication. Plan quotas limit active automations.

## Best practice

Keep workflows short, name steps clearly, and verify sending resources before activation. Automations do not bypass email or WhatsApp provider limits.
