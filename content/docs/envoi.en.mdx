title: Sending, Domains, and Senders
description: Configure sending identity and protect deliverability.

## Domains

Sending domains are managed through Resend. MailPulse displays the required DNS records, including SPF, DKIM, and DMARC depending on the provider response.

## Verification

A domain must be verified before it can be used as the main sending resource. Without a verified domain, some features may use the MailPulse fallback when `MAILPULSE_MANAGED_FROM_EMAIL` is configured.

## Senders

Senders attach an email address to an available domain. They are used by campaigns and Filon recovery emails.

## Limits

A misconfigured domain hurts deliverability. DNS changes can take time depending on the registrar and public caches.
